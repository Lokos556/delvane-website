AUTONOMOUS OPERATIONS

Autonomous operations. From booking to administration.

From reservation synchronisation to guest and staff communication, self check-in, government reporting, billing, taxes and turnover, Delvane AI runs configured workflows autonomously.

01 / BEFORE ARRIVAL

Reservations trigger the workflow.

When a booking arrives, Delvane AI prepares the required workflow, sends the cleaner the property and timing details, and coordinates the hand-off automatically.

02 / DURING THE STAY

Messages are handled automatically.

Delvane AI answers guest questions using their reservation context through email, WhatsApp and connected booking channels such as Booking.com, then takes the appropriate next step.

03 / AFTER DEPARTURE

Tasks are coordinated to completion.

Delvane AI coordinates cleaners and maintenance workers, schedules turnover tasks, follows completion and prepares the property for the next arrival without requiring the owner to review every step.

THE PRINCIPLE

One reservation.
Every next step handled.

  1. Capture
