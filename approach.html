LESS FRICTION. MORE PRESENCE.

Less friction. More presence.

Delvane is built around the real rhythm of independent hospitality: preparing for a stay, being there while it happens, and getting ready for the next welcome.

01 / BEFORE ARRIVAL

Know what needs to happen.

Review the reservation, check the guest and stay details, and turn the upcoming arrival into a clear preparation plan. A cleaner should not have to hunt through a chat to know which apartment to prepare.

02 / DURING THE STAY

Answer with context.

When a guest asks about check-in or a special request, the useful information is the message plus the stay it belongs to. Delvane is designed to keep that context close and make unanswered conversations easier to spot.

03 / AFTER DEPARTURE

Close the loop.

Coordinate cleaning, mark readiness, note maintenance, and make sure the next arrival does not inherit an unfinished task. The goal is a visible hand-off, not another message asking whether it was done.

THE PRINCIPLE

One reservation.
Connected follow-through.

  1. Capture

    Bring the booking and guest details into view.

  2. Understand

    Connect the stay to its messages, dates, property, and requests.

  3. Act

    Make the next task visible and coordinate who owns it.

  4. Confirm

    Track readiness and unresolved items before the next guest arrives.
